# Individual spell art

200 original AI-generated 192px icons: eight arcane spells and 24 spells for each of the eight Winds of Magic lores. Artwork only; book rules and illustrations are not redistributed.

Resolved by normalized English title or original image basename, before the existing school fallback. Apostrophes, accents and separators do not affect matching. Localized titles need a matching source image basename; otherwise the school icon remains. Disabling item icons preserves original artwork. Game documents are not rewritten.

This is the book catalogue, not a verified complete world inventory: petty magic, rituals and custom spells are outside this set. Craft-result artwork is distributed by Recipe Book.
